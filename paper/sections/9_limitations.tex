We frame the present results as a focused study of a single objective, and several limitations bound their interpretation.

\textbf{No head-to-head baselines.} We do not compare against the methods we position against: feature-alignment objectives such as REPA/VIRAL~\citep{yurepresentation, yoon2025visual}, inference-time attention-steering heuristics, or Chefer et al.'s attention supervision in unimodal ViTs~\citep{chefer2022optimizing}. Our claims concern the \emph{effect of the objective} and the intrinsic--downstream dissociation, not state-of-the-art performance; a controlled comparison to these alternatives is needed to position the method quantitatively.

\textbf{Intrinsic metrics are in-distribution and partly circular.} AMR, AP, and NSS are computed on held-out images but in the same COCONut distribution and annotation style as training, so they measure faithful localization rather than out-of-distribution generalization. Moreover all three reward in-mask concentration, and AMR is exactly the test-time form of the training target; they should be read as verifying that optimization succeeded, with the downstream suite---not the intrinsic metrics---carrying the burden of showing usefulness.

\textbf{Limited experimental scope and significance.} Results are for one architecture (LLaVA-1.5-7B), one loss weight ($\lambda{=}0.5$), $200$ optimization steps, and a single seed. The downstream differences in \Cref{tab:bench} are at the $1$--$2$ point level and we do not claim statistical significance; cross-seed stability, a $\lambda$ sweep, and longer schedules are untested, as is the squared-error variant of \Cref{eq:align}.

\textbf{Mechanistic claims are correlational.} The ``where it adapts'' analysis rests on parameter drift, a proxy for functional change. We identify candidate localization heads (including layer~11/query/head~22) by how much their weights move, but do not causally ablate them; the attractor's reproducibility is shown across training configurations, not across seeds.

\textbf{Qualitative and failure-mode evidence is illustrative.} \Cref{fig:qual} reports two representative tokens rather than a quantitative study, and the background-``stuff'' degradation, while consistent in our inspection, is not measured at scale---in part because the grounding annotations that would define a stuff-localization metric are exactly what is missing.
